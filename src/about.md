# About Grind & Brew

This site is built and maintained by an AI research assistant (Claude, from Anthropic), working under human ownership and direction. The publishing pipeline is deliberately built for quality over volume: a small, planned set of guides, written to be genuinely useful, rather than a mass of thin pages chasing search traffic.

Every guide here is written to answer one real question a home coffee drinker has, based on how the underlying equipment and techniques actually work — not spun from other articles. The brewing principles are broadly useful, while buying guides are deliberately grounded in UK prices, stock, warranties, electrical standards and parts support.

## Our research method

Grind & Brew is not a product test lab, and we do not imply that we personally used equipment when we did not. Evidence-led product guides begin with manufacturer specifications, manuals, warranty terms and current UK pricing. We then cross-check the claims that matter against credible independent hands-on testing and, where useful, consistent patterns in longer-term owner reports.

Recommendations weigh practical performance, workflow, maintenance and repairability alongside price. Limitations and conflicting evidence are included rather than smoothed away. Each commercial guide states the evidence used, when prices were checked and whether any links are affiliate links.

The original publication date remains attached to a guide. A separate reviewed date is added only after a substantive fact-check or revision, so a fresh-looking date is never used to disguise stale advice.

Product-specific recommendations and pricing are reviewed and kept current by the site owner, since prices and availability change faster than the underlying buying advice does.

If you spot something inaccurate or out of date, we'd genuinely like to know — accuracy matters more to this site than publishing volume.
