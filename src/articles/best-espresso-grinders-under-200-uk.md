For proper espresso, “grinds fine” is not enough. You need small, repeatable adjustments so you can tune a shot without jumping past the sweet spot. That rules out a lot of general-purpose grinders.

## What “under £200” does and does not buy

All three can grind for a normal, unpressurised espresso basket. What you do not get is near-infinite adjustment, whisper-quiet grinding or a heavy metal build. Light roasts will expose those limits sooner than medium or dark espresso roasts.

If maximum grind quality per pound matters more than convenience, a good hand grinder can outperform these electric choices because your budget pays for burrs and alignment rather than a motor. The trade-off is real: grinding an espresso dose by hand every morning can become tiresome, especially with lighter beans or several drinks in a row.

## Which one should you buy?

- Choose the **Baratza Encore ESP** if espresso quality, simple dialling-in and repairability come first.
- Choose the **Fellow Opus** if you single-dose, switch brew methods often and care about a clean, compact counter setup.
- Choose the **Sage Dose Control Pro** if you keep beans in the hopper and want timed, direct-to-portafilter convenience.

For most buyers, start with the Encore ESP. It is not flawless—it simply offers the best mix of control, price and long-term repairability.
