If you want unpressurised-basket espresso, the grinder has to do more than simply produce fine coffee. It needs small, repeatable adjustments so you can slow down or speed up a shot without jumping straight past the useful setting. That requirement removes many general-purpose burr grinders from this list.

## The short answer

The **Baratza Encore ESP is our strongest all-round recommendation**. At its current £159.95 manufacturer price, it combines a genuinely fine espresso adjustment range, simple controls, an included dosing cup and unusually good access to replacement parts. The Fellow Opus is the neater single-dose option if appearance and low mess matter more than effortless dialling-in. The Sage Dose Control Pro is the practical hopper-fed choice for households that value timed, direct-to-portafilter dosing.

Why it wins: Baratza assigns settings 1–20 to a higher-resolution espresso range, while 21–40 cover broader brew methods. The manufacturer lists 40mm M2 steel conical burrs, a two-year warranty and a quick-release burr mount. CoffeeGeek's hands-on testing measured roughly 20-micron changes per click in the espresso range and found it easier to dial in than the original Encore, while also flagging clumpy espresso output, retention and the lack of a timer. Those are meaningful drawbacks, but none undermines its central job: making repeatable espresso adjustments at this price.

## What “under £200” does and does not buy

All three picks can grind finely enough for a conventional, unpressurised espresso basket. None gives you the near-infinite adjustment, low retention, quietness or heavy metal build found on more expensive specialist grinders. Light-roast espresso is also the hardest test: it may expose the limits of stepped adjustment and small conical burrs sooner than a traditional medium or dark espresso roast.

If maximum grind quality per pound matters more than convenience, a good hand grinder can outperform these electric choices because your budget pays for burrs and alignment rather than a motor. The trade-off is real: grinding an espresso dose by hand every morning can become tiresome, especially with lighter beans or several drinks in a row.

## Which one should you buy?

- Choose the **Baratza Encore ESP** if espresso quality, simple dialling-in and repairability come first.
- Choose the **Fellow Opus** if you single-dose, switch brew methods often and care about a clean, compact counter setup.
- Choose the **Sage Dose Control Pro** if you keep beans in the hopper and want timed, direct-to-portafilter convenience.

For most buyers, start with the Encore ESP. The recommendation is not based on a claim that it is flawless; it is based on the balance of espresso adjustment, current UK price, independent test evidence and long-term serviceability.
